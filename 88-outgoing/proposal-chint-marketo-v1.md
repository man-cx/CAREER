## Slide 5 — Four motions, four tracks

| Motion | Typical customers | What gets automated |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Distribution / channel** | Distributors, dealers, regional partners | Partner onboarding, certification cadence, regional co-marketing, reactivation |
| **Utility / grid** | National power companies, grid projects | ABM + tender-stage triggers, spec-content nurture, reference-case deployment |
| **Industrial / OEM** | OEMs, process industries, data centers | Buying-group mapping, solution-content journeys by vertical, design-in tracking |

Each track has its own lead stages, scoring rules, and routing logic — because a distributor lead and a grid-project lead are not the same thing.
