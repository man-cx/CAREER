# 2026 Marketo Vendor RFP — CHINT

## 1. Project Background

Since 2023, CHINT has been progressively deploying the Marketo marketing automation platform, aiming to build a global, standardized digital marketing system that enables end-to-end closed-loop management — from lead acquisition and nurturing to distribution and sales conversion.

As of 2026, Marketo's full closed-loop system has taken initial shape, with adoption across 22 countries and multiple business lines. Through this phase, CHINT has established unified global marketing standards, processes, and data frameworks. However, as the business scales and regional needs diverge, challenges remain in platform depth of use and data governance.

## 2. Pain Points

### 2.1 Broken Lead Tracking — ROI Difficult to Assess

- **Incomplete system integration:** While technical integration with CRM exists, most countries have not yet enabled CRM, and sales staff only use the system at the quotation stage, resulting in a lack of tracking for downstream lead conversion.
- **Product-line divergence:** High-voltage products have long sales cycles and complex chains; low-voltage leads are often passed to distributors with unclear tracking paths.
- **Low sales feedback rate:** Email feedback rates from sales are low due to multi-tier lead handoffs (high/medium/low voltage lines), insufficient sales awareness, and low lead interest — making closed-loop tracking difficult.

### 2.2 Subsidiary Digital Marketing Capability Gaps

- **Low usage awareness:** Some subsidiaries do not integrate Marketo into daily marketing, treating it only as a task assigned by HQ.
- **Uneven regional adoption — three maturity tiers:**
  1. No awareness — essentially not using the platform
  2. Email-only — mass Newsletter sends
  3. Automation applied to specific campaigns (very few)

### 2.3 HQ Marketing Automation Strategy Lacks Depth

- **Weak precision marketing:**
  HQ still operates primarily through one-off campaign execution, lacking cross-cycle, tiered automated journey design. Customer behaviour and historical interaction data are underused for dynamic content recommendations and personalised outreach.
  Form design lacks business-tiering logic: forms are not differentiated by product line or marketing stage; high-potential and general enquiry customers are treated identically. The customer tagging system is immature.

- **Immature lead scoring and behavioural triggers:**
  - Initial scoring models lack data-driven validation; scoring is highly subjective and not optimised against real conversion data.
  - Imbalance between behavioural and profile scoring — behavioural actions do not reliably reflect purchase intent.
  - Is a lead scoring model appropriate at this stage?
  - Should scoring models differ by region?

- **No unified subsidiary lead distribution:**
  Subsidiaries lack a standardised lead distribution method. Downstream lead tracking breaks, making it unclear whether Marcom has followed up or forwarded to sales.

### 2.4 Data Governance Is Time-Consuming

- **Poor data standardisation:** Subsidiaries upload data without following unified field standards; data is chaotic; garbled characters in non-Latin languages.
- **Difficulty in cross-system bi-directional data synchronisation and troubleshooting**
- **Feishu-based dashboards are strained as lead volume grows**
- **Data compliance**

## 3. Project Objectives

### 3.1 Strengthen the Data Foundation — Build a Visual Customer Profile System

- Establish a unified, standardised global data governance framework to ensure lead data is clean, accurate, and traceable — providing a reliable foundation for marketing automation.
- Improve the customer tagging system and field standards; integrate multi-dimensional data (source, behaviour, industry, interest) to form a 360-degree customer profile.
- Enable visualised management of marketing and sales data, providing precise segmentation and targeting for campaigns, and valuable customer insights and opportunity assessments for the sales team.

### 3.2 Close the Process Loop — Build a Marketing Automation & Precision Marketing System

- Connect the full lead journey (capture → nurture → score → distribute → sales feedback) to achieve marketing-to-sales data linkage and closed-loop tracking.
- Optimise automation strategies and lead scoring models; deliver personalised outreach and tiered nurturing based on customer behaviour and profile. Drive the shift from one-off campaign execution to long-term, data-driven intelligent marketing operations.
- Establish a unified lead distribution and tracking mechanism, with every lead's routing path clearly traceable.

### 3.3 Deepen Regional Enablement — Improve Subsidiaries' Autonomous Operations

- Tiered training to systematically lift each regional team's digital marketing awareness and practical capability.
- **Already deployed (22 countries):** Deepen application scenarios, promote best-practice cases, drive the shift from "HQ-driven" to "autonomous operation."
- **To be deployed:** Complete platform initialisation and data integration; provide online guidance and onboarding training to build foundational digital operations capability.
