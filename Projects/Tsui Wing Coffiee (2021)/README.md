# Tsui Wing Coffiee (2021)

Tsit Wing Group, a Hong Kong coffee and beverage supplier, engaged Novus Consulting in 2021 to build a SAP customer-relationship management system for its sales teams, with Man Li serving as Novus's Solution Lead on the engagement. The work ran from proposal in September 2021 through rollout and user testing in 2022, with a second phase planned for a customer self-order portal.
