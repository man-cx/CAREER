# Suunto (2022-23)

Suunto is a Finnish maker of sports watches and outdoor instruments. From late 2022 into 2023, BORN Group / Tech Mahindra built Suunto a new SAP customer-experience platform — joining up customer data, marketing campaigns and customer service — then carried it into support. Man led the engagement as PM / Lead Architect at BORN.
