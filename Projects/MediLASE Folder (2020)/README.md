# MediLASE (2020)

MediLASE is a Hong Kong medical aesthetics clinic group (laser hair removal and skin treatments). From late 2019 into 2021, Prizm Data Tech and SAP delivered its integrated marketing solution — a SAP Marketing Cloud build plus the customer analytics, reports and campaigns that ran on it. Man Li's role on the engagement was **Project Director** (credited on the Coupon Management deck, Aug 2021).
